Privacy Policy

Last updated · July 2026

01 — Overview

Introduction

Welcome to SubsTrack. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our iOS application.

By using SubsTrack, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02 — Data

Information We Collect

Data you provide:

  • Name and amount of your subscriptions
  • Billing dates and payment cycles
  • Categories and notes you assign

Automatically collected data:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads — only if you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • Anonymous usage data to improve the app experience

03 — Usage

How We Use Your Information

  • To display your subscription summary and spending insights
  • To send you upcoming billing notifications
  • To show relevant ads to free-tier users via Google AdMob
  • To improve app performance and experience

04 — Storage

Data Storage

All your subscription data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store your financial data on external servers.

Widget data is shared between the app and the widget using iOS App Groups — this data never leaves your device.

💡 SubsTrack does not have a backend server. Your data stays on your iPhone.

05 — Advertising

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SubsTrack shows ads to free-tier users via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to personalize ads according to their own privacy policies.

You can permanently remove all ads by purchasing SubsTrack Premium.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy ↗

06 — Purchases

In-App Purchases

SubsTrack offers a one-time purchase (Premium — $1.99) processed entirely by Apple App Store. We do not store, see, or have access to any payment data.

See Apple's Privacy Policy ↗ for purchase-related details.

07 — Children

Children's Privacy

SubsTrack is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

08 — Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify you through the app or by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of SubsTrack after changes implies your acceptance.
